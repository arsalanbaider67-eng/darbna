Darbna is a free navigation app for Iraq (Arabic first, Sorani Kurdish and English). No account or sign-in is required.

TESTING OUTSIDE IRAQ
Allow foreground location, or use the labelled Erbil tour option when outside Iraq or location is unavailable (a fixed public tour position, not the user's location). Search for "Erbil Citadel" or "Erbil airport" and press the full-search button; typing shows local suggestions. Pick a place, request directions and press Start. The labelled Demo mode simulates driving so guidance and voice can be tried without moving. Tour/demo positions cannot send road reports or traffic samples.

SHARE TO DARBNA
From Apple Maps, Google Maps or a chat app, use Share > Darbna on a place or map link. Darbna opens, reads the location and offers directions.

AUDIO
Arabic guidance uses bundled recorded Standard Arabic prompts; English prompts are also bundled. Settings has a voice test.

LOCATION / CONNECTIVITY
Navigation and trip sharing run in the foreground only; no background location is requested. Routes, search and community features need connectivity.

COMMUNITY REPORTS (user-generated content)
Fixed road-condition categories only: no profiles, chat, photos or free text in the report feed. A report warns other drivers only after a second driver confirms it. Every report has "Not there", "Wrong or abusive report" (flag) and "Block this reporter". Flags hide a report; blocking hides all reports from that sender for the user. The developer reviews flags and bans senders after five confirmed fake/abusive reports. Contact: darbnaiq@gmail.com. Reporting and confirming are disabled while the phone is moving unless the user confirms they are a passenger.
Checkpoint reports show waiting times at permanent security checkpoints; the app does not route around checkpoints, does not show police patrols and has no DUI checkpoint feature. SOS opens the phone dialler for 911 only after the user taps it; please do not call it.

POINTS AND PRIZES (Guideline 5.3)
Drivers earn +2 points when another driver confirms their report and +2 for confirming another driver's report. Every 10 levels (500 points) the driver wins a prize (phone credit, voucher, free oil change or cash), chosen and paid by the developer. It is free (no purchase or in-app payment), not a draw, 18+ and Iraq residents only, and must be claimed within 7 days by contacting support with the code shown in the app. Official rules are in the app (Settings > Prizes > Full rules) and online: https://arsalanbaider67-eng.github.io/darbna/prize-rules.html. The rules state that Apple is not a sponsor and is not involved in any way. A new installation starts at level 1, so the reviewer will see the progress bar and rules but not a won prize.

PRIVACY
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Traffic contribution is off by default and needs an explicit choice. Reports use a random installation code. Settings > Delete my data removes reports, votes, points, prizes and shared trips after server confirmation.
