# Darbna brand (guide v1.0, 11 Oct 2026)

`final/` holds the production artwork used by the app. The logo outlines (symbol, دربنا and Darbna
wordmarks) are taken unchanged from the vector cover of the approved *Darbna brand guide v1.0* PDF;
nothing was redrawn or retyped. Regenerate the PNGs from those outlines only.

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Darbna Purple | `#21194A` | backgrounds (**app panels and screens**), icon tile, **your arrow and route on the map** |
| Darbna Yellow | `#FFD25E` | symbol accent, highlights, CTAs — only on purple, never on white |
| Darbna White | `#FFF5EF` | wordmark and **app text** on purple, light backgrounds |

Files: `ios-app-store-1024.png` (opaque), `google-play-512.png`, Android adaptive foreground and
monochrome (432 px, symbol inside the 66 dp safe zone, background `#21194A`), favicons 16–512,
`social-avatar-1080.png` (circle-safe), lockups (primary on purple, all-purple on white), SVG masters.

The thmanyah Sans font may not be web-embedded or shipped under its licence, so the app keeps
Noto Sans Arabic / the system font for UI text; the logo itself is always artwork.
